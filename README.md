# White-wine-quality-analysis-using-IBM-SPSS
Improving White Wine Quality and Inventory Management  

Project Focus:

1. Predictive Modeling for Quality Assessment: Implementing regression and classification models to predict white wine quality based on chemical attributes, enabling proactive inventory selection.
   
2. Inventory Optimization: Prioritizing stocking white wines with chemical profiles aligned with higher quality scores to optimize inventory within space constraints.

3.Customer Satisfaction: Aligning inventory with quality predictions to increase customer satisfaction and loyalty, leading to improved sales and profitability.

Skills: Business Analysis · IBM SPSS
